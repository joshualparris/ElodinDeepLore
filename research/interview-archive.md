# Elodin Interview Archive

Working archive of direct or near-direct author commentary related to Elodin.

## 1. WIRED Book Club excerpt on the official Rothfuss interviews page

- Source: <https://www.patrickrothfuss.com/content/interviews.html>
- Status: direct official source
- Relevant lines: roughly 95-99 on the current page scrape

What it gives us:
- Rothfuss says a lot of Elodin's teaching technique came from old Zen masters and Buddhists.
- His explanation is about leading someone toward sudden intuitive understanding rather than explaining the thing directly.

Why it matters:
- This is the clearest author-side explanation of why Elodin teaches through destabilization, indirection, and apparent nonsense.

## 2. Dragonpage note preserved on the official Rothfuss interviews page

- Source: <https://www.patrickrothfuss.com/content/interviews.html>
- Status: official secondary note pointing to older audio
- Relevant lines: roughly 45-48 on the current page scrape
- Official note: the site says Rothfuss mentions, about sixteen minutes into the podcast, that Elodin is actually teaching Kvothe while Kvothe does not even know it.

Why it matters:
- Even before we recover the original audio cleanly, the official site itself preserves the substance of the Elodin point.
- It aligns closely with the `Wise Man's Fear` Hemme-room sequence.

## 3. Worldbuilders 2016 pronunciation clip

- Source: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MPEB6NAGoYk&t=294s>

Why it matters:
- In a lore atlas built around names, pronunciation references are worth keeping.
