## Strongest design conclusions

- Book two makes Elodin much more central.
- He is still destabilizing, but now visibly protective in selective, deliberate ways.
- He understands Haven as a place of real harm for the wrong person.
- He recognizes naming quality in Kvothe even when Kvothe cannot explain what he has done.
- His teaching remains oblique, but `Wise Man's Fear` makes it much harder to call it random.
